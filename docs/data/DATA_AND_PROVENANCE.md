# Evidence and provenance

The pilot treats UniBE, ARTORG and Diabetes Center Berne public pages and public
research PDFs as one ecosystem. The supplied workbook is a discovery inventory,
not proof of complete acquisition. [Materials](../provided-material/README.md)
records its actual rows and caveats.

## Source lifecycle

Register source/family and canonical URL; observe fetch/redirect/status/date;
store raw and normalized artifact hashes; create immutable document versions;
map chunks to exact evidence spans. Preserve Unicode offsets, heading/page context,
source language, fetched time and nullable publication date. Unknown publication
dates must not be replaced by fetch/ingest timestamps. Canonical aliases deduplicate
URLs without merging unrelated people or conflicting source versions.

A source-backed claim connects a typed subject to an entity or typed literal,
with exact support links and derivation. Validate:
`body[char_start:char_end] == literal_text`. IDs, hashes, scopes and membership are
code checks; semantic support requires assessment of the complete statement.
Source review status, claim support and company relevance are separate dimensions.

## Case evidence

Runs pin corpus membership/build and model/taxonomy configuration. New web evidence
is unreviewed and case-local, even when its claim is supported. Duplicate URLs
prefer the pinned corpus version; mirrors retain original IDs in lineage. Query
scores and search snippets never substitute for source passages. Revocation is
checked on reads and output eligibility, including historical artifacts.

Missing roles, contacts, projects or infrastructure remain unknown. Adjacent
applications retain uncertainty. Source support cannot establish expert availability,
willingness to collaborate, regulatory approval or clinical suitability.

## Input and rights

Operators read confidential company documents outside the app and type permitted
context only. Such context remains attributed, not public-source evidence.
Source pages retain institutional copyright; code licensing does not relicense
source text, model weights, personal data or recordings. Use partner logo/template
only for this challenge. Original transcripts inform requirements and never enter
the scientific evidence corpus. Privacy/retention/access decisions must support
nDSG and applicable GDPR requirements without claiming legal certification.
