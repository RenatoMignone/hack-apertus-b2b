# Product requirements

The operator is Bern economic-development staff translating a company's plans
into a concise, inspectable opportunity brief for company decision-makers.
The [supplied challenge](CHALLENGE_BRIEF.md) and [workshop evidence](../recordings/RECORDING_INSIGHTS.md)
define needs; [architecture](../plans/ARCHITECTURE.md) defines the implementation.

## Core workflow

1. Enter permitted company context, activity and objectives in Bern after reading
   company documents outside the app.
2. Resolve scientific terminology through versioned domain taxonomy and structured
   interpretation, preserving original intent and uncertainty.
3. Retrieve across UniBE, ARTORG and DCB as one ecosystem; optionally supplement
   with flagged public web research.
4. Present relevant people/units, affiliations, expertise, projects and infrastructure
   where source-supported, with a short match explanation and original source links.
5. Inspect passages, refine through chat, review every reason and export kept
   findings in a concise Why Bern brief.

## Acceptance principles

Every public assertion must be traceable to an exact authorized source passage.
No invented identities, roles, contacts, projects or inferred willingness to
collaborate. Source review, semantic support and company relevance are distinct.
An adjacent opportunity is labeled; absent information stays unknown. Insufficient
intent triggers clarification; insufficient evidence produces honest partial/no-match
coverage rather than an invented answer.

Confidential plans remain outside every model. Operator context remains attributed.
New sources can be added via registry/profile/build configuration without replacing
the graph. Required source coverage and the 80% top-10 expert/unit target must be
[evaluated](EVALUATION.md), not inferred from demos. English is the baseline;
multilingual quality needs separate evidence.
