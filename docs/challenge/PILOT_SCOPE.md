# Pilot scope

The initial domain is **diabetes technology in Bern**, covering UniBE, ARTORG and
Diabetes Center Berne as one distributed ecosystem. Public institutional pages
and public research PDFs supply evidence. The workbook seeds discovery; it is
not exhaustive or evidence of topic relevance. Source-family membership can overlap.

Queries connect company needs such as glucose sensing, continuous monitoring,
insulin delivery, biosensing and related biomedical engineering to supported people,
units, projects, facilities and services. Similar terms are query expansions;
scientific equivalence and company fit must still be assessed.

Domain profiles version vocabulary, fields, aliases, source scope and compatible
retrieval configuration. Other domains require reviewed sources/taxonomy and a
compatible build; new labels alone do not establish new coverage. See
[profiles](../data/DOMAIN_PROFILES.md).

## Evaluation decision

Use unique typed person/unit IDs in a shared top ten, with English intent-balanced
macro Recall@10 ≥ 0.80 as the internal operational interpretation of the challenge
target. Average wording variants per intent, then average intents; report per-type,
per-variant and micro diagnostics. This aggregation is a team decision, not an
organizer-specified formula. Broad requests require reviewed expected sets.
Candidate queries are unlabelled until independently reviewed. Source completeness,
claim support and DE/FR/IT quality are separate measurements.
