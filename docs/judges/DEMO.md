# Case walkthrough

[Setup and run modes](QUICKSTART.md): seeded UI, synthetic backend, or live corpus
and providers. Seeded examples use scripted results; live results depend on the
available sources and configured services.

## Example request

> HelioSense is a fictional company developing wearable glucose sensors. It seeks
> research capabilities in Bern for sensing algorithms and evaluation methods.
> Identify relevant groups and infrastructure, distinguishing direct evidence
> from adjacent capabilities. Do not assume availability for collaboration.

## Workflow

1. Open **New case**, enter the request and select **Start research**.
2. Follow research progress and answer any blocking clarification in chat.
3. Read the reason cards, direct/adjacent labels and coverage limitations.
4. Open a citation to inspect the exact passage and original URL. **Pinned corpus**
   and **New case research** distinguish source provenance.
5. Keep or reject every card. Rejection excludes a card from the report; it does
   not mark the source as false.
6. Select **Export**, approve the current report and selection, and download the
   PDF. Export requires all cards reviewed and at least one kept. PPTX is available
   only when enabled by the backend.
7. **Rewrite existing report** changes wording using the frozen evidence pack.
   **Research / change focus** starts a new research revision.
8. Open history to read earlier revisions. Changed content requires a new review.

Missing people, roles, facilities and contacts remain unknown. Source relevance,
factual support and willingness to collaborate are separate findings.
