# Case brief — HelioSense (fictional demo company)

> **Simulation input only.** HelioSense and every business detail below are
> fictional. This is not a real company, investment proposal, clinical product,
> or statement about any University of Bern institution.

HelioSense is a fictional early-stage Swiss medtech company exploring a
wearable system for continuous glucose monitoring. The team wants to understand
which capabilities in the Bern research ecosystem could be relevant to an
early, non-clinical research and development collaboration.

Please identify source-supported people and units at UniBE, ARTORG and the
Diabetes Center Berne whose documented work is relevant to one or more of these
areas: glucose sensing or monitoring, biosensors, wearable or point-of-care
measurement, diabetes technology, signal processing, device development,
preclinical evaluation, or clinical research infrastructure. Include relevant
projects, facilities, services, or contact routes only when an institutional
source explicitly documents them.

For each match, explain the connection in plain language and distinguish a
directly documented match from an adjacent capability. Cite the exact public
source pages supporting each person, role, affiliation, expertise, project or
service. Do not infer that a person is available, willing to collaborate, or a
contact person unless the source says so. Do not claim that a technology is
clinically validated, compatible with a glucose-monitoring product, or ready
for transfer unless the cited source explicitly establishes that.

The scenario is deliberately exploratory: do not assume that the company has a
validated prototype, clinical data, regulatory approval, funding, or a confirmed
Bern partner. If the indexed pages do not support a requested detail, mark it
unknown and state the coverage limitation. Keep the fictional company context
separate from public facts about institutions and researchers.

**Desired output:** a concise, evidence-backed shortlist for a first internal
discussion, with source links and clear uncertainty. This is not an invitation
or a claim that any researcher has agreed to meet.
